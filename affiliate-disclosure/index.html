Affiliate disclosure

Narton is an independent affiliate publication. We have a commercial relationship with the retailer we feature and can benefit financially when you buy through our links.

Effective November 21, 2026

Why we disclose this

The products in this catalog are promoted through a commercial relationship. Our selection and placement of products may reflect that relationship. The guide explains what is in a download and shows excerpts so you can make your own choice.

What the buttons do

Add to cart opens the retailer’s cart with the selected download. Buy now opens the retailer’s checkout with that item selected. A referral label identifies this site as the source of the visit. You can review the retailer’s total and terms before placing an order.

Who handles the purchase

Narton publishes product information. The retailer sets the price, takes payment, issues receipts, delivers files and decides refund requests under its policies and applicable law. Use the contact details on your receipt for help with an order.

Product information and corrections

We use product specifications and file previews to prepare these guides. Prices and availability are a catalog snapshot and can change. If a listing appears out of date, send us the page address and the correction.
