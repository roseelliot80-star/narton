Site policies

Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit narton.shop, search the guides, email the publication or follow a shopping link.

Effective November 21, 2026

Visiting the site

Your browser requests pages and images from our hosting and delivery providers. Those services may process an IP address, browser or device details, requested page, referring page, and request time for delivery, diagnostics and security. A landing-page URL may include a campaign parameter from the ad or link you clicked.

Search and site features

Catalog search and collection filters run in your browser. Narton does not send the text you type into the catalog search to a search provider. A search included in the page address can appear in your browser history or hosting logs when that address is requested.

The site has no visitor accounts or newsletter list. Its pages do not load advertising pixels, third-party analytics, embedded video or remote fonts.

When you email us

If you contact Narton, your email provider sends us your address, the subject, message and any attachments you choose to include. We and our email provider use that information to answer your request, investigate a correction and maintain the related correspondence. Please leave payment details and sensitive personal information out of your message.

Shopping and referrals

Purchase buttons link to an external retailer. A link includes a site-level referral label identifying Narton as the source. The retailer and its service providers handle their own cookies, cart, payment, order emails and delivery. The retailer may provide referral reports so sales can be attributed to this publication. Review its privacy policy before entering payment or account details.

Retention and requests

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Service providers may retain technical logs under their own operational schedules.

To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent directly to Narton, email support@narton.shop with enough detail to identify your correspondence. We may ask you to confirm control of the relevant email address. Requests about a retailer’s order records belong with that retailer.

Children and changes

The site is written for adults choosing downloadable products, including activities for children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. A parent or guardian can contact us about information sent by a child.

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
