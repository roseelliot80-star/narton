The printing guide

Before you press print

A quick guide to choosing the right file, getting the page size right and printing the sheets you actually want to use.

Match the PDF to your paper

US Letter measures 8.5 × 11 inches. A4 measures 210 × 297 millimeters. They are close enough to look alike on screen, but a page set up for one size may be scaled or clipped on the other.

If a download includes both sizes, open the file matching the paper in your printer. The Christmas planner contains the same 70 designs in both formats.

Run a one-page test

Open the print dialog and select the same paper size there. Start at Actual size or 100% when the PDF matches your paper. Print a single worksheet and check the margins and writing space before sending the whole file. If your printer clips an edge, try Fit and compare the result.

Choose pages before printing

Use the PDF thumbnails to pick a page range. A coloring book may include blank backs by design. A planner may include annual pages you only need once and weekly pages you want to reuse within your license.

Keep a clean digital copy

Extract ZIP files first. Save an untouched copy of the original PDF and open a duplicate in a PDF annotation app if you want to write on a tablet. Clickable contents are available only in products whose guide lists that feature.
