Links from ads

An ad platform may add campaign information to the address that brought you here. Its own tracking and cookie settings apply to activity on that platform. This site does not load an ad platform’s pixel or copy your incoming ad identifiers into purchase links.

When you open a shopping link

The shopping link carries a Narton referral label in its URL. After you leave this site, the retailer may use cookies to keep items in a cart, operate checkout, measure referrals and protect the transaction. Its cookie notice and controls apply on its pages.

Your controls

Your browser settings let you inspect, delete or block cookies and clear cached files. Blocking the retailer’s necessary cookies can affect cart or checkout functions on its website.
