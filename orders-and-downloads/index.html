Orders, downloads & refunds

Narton helps you choose a digital product. The retailer named at checkout is the seller and handles your order from payment through delivery.

Effective November 21, 2026

Before you order

Check the product’s file type, edition, paper size and license. The products featured here are digital downloads. Desks, printed books, binders and other props in the editorial photography are for illustration.

Payment and digital delivery

Your cart and payment page are on the retailer’s website. Use the download instructions in its confirmation page or order email. If a ZIP is supplied, extract the files before opening the PDFs or images.

A missing link or file

Check the email address used at checkout, your spam folder and the confirmation page. For a missing link, incorrect item or damaged file, contact the retailer through your receipt. Include the order reference and a short description of the problem.

Cancellations and refunds

The retailer’s published terms and applicable consumer protections govern eligibility and time limits for cancellations and refunds. Review those terms before purchase. Send requests to the retailer through your order confirmation so it can locate the transaction and respond.

An error on a Narton page

For a broken purchase link or incorrect file specification in this guide, send the Narton page address to support@narton.shop. Please keep banking details and payment card numbers out of your message.
